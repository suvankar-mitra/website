Professional experience.

Technology Lead

SME and technical owner for the data snapshotting domain within the Global Order Settlement System (GLOSS), a long-running financial platform in Private Wealth Management.

  • Owned a two-dimensional temporal snapshotting model tracking settlement/account state by business-date validity and intraday snapshot timestamp, supporting current-date and as-of processing.
  • Designed and maintained snapshot versioning and milestoning so historical state remained available across snapshot windows.

  • Modernized 100+ legacy services, developed Java/Shell/SQL/C tooling, optimized stored procedures, reduced operational support incidents by 35%, and delivered GitLab CI/CD pipelines.
